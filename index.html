About Me

I turn data into impactful solutions.

With hands-on experience across data analysis, machine learning, and dashboard development, I help businesses make informed decisions through data-driven strategies and efficient, well-documented analytical pipelines from raw data to business-ready insight. My journey into data has never been just about learning tools or building dashboards. I’ve worked across operations, client management, technical support, project coordination and digital interfaces.
